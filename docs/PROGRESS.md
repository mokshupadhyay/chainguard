**Subscription-spam fix (this change):** the wildcard-emitter policy subscription (any Base Sepolia contract could make `react()` run at our lREACT cost) is gone. `ChainGuardReactive` now trusts one factory (`setFactory`, admin once), registers Guards from the factory's `GuardCreated` (which now carries the initial policy), and subscribes at runtime inside `react()` to each Guard's policy events and to `Approval` on allowlisted tokens with `owner = guard`. Only registered Guards can influence policy or trigger revocation; `maxGuards` and ≤ 8 tokens bound the work. Details and residual risks: docs/DEPLOYMENT.md §C, docs/ARCHITECTURE.md.
**Registration readiness (this change):** after registering a Guard, `react()` sends a `confirmRegistered(reactive, tokens[])` callback to it. Until it lands, `Guard.ready()` is false and `approveToken`/`setPolicy` revert `NotProtected` (withdraw and manual revoke still work); after it, `approveToken` rejects tokens outside the confirmed allowlist (`UnsupportedToken`). The frontend shows a two-step registration status (registered on Lasna → confirmed on Base), lists the protected tokens, explains unsupported tokens/NFTs, and disables protected actions until ready. `live-loop.sh` waits on `guard.ready()` instead of a fixed sleep. Caveat: the callback is requested right after the `subscribe` calls in the same `react()`, which is the best on-chain signal available but not proof the subscriptions are active; the live run verifies it. Not yet committed.

**Interface changes:** `ChainGuardReactive(originChainId, tokens[], maxGuards)` (no `watchToken`, no constructor subscriptions); `GuardFactory.GuardCreated(owner, guard, enabled, revokeUnlimited, maxAllowance)`; new deploy step `setFactory` between factory deploy and first Guard.
